# First West Credit Union (Tru Cooperative Bank)

> Member-owned Canadian cooperative financial institution headquartered in Langley, British Columbia, rebranded Tru Cooperative Bank in 2026 upon receiving a federal charter under Canada's Bank Act. Roughly 291,000 members, ~$20.8B in total assets, 45 branches, ~1,300 employees, operating the Envision Financial, Valley First, Island Savings, and Enderby & District Financial regional brands over a shared network. CDIC-insured.

## API posture

- First West / Tru runs **no first-party public developer portal** and publishes **no downloadable OpenAPI, Swagger, or partner API**.
- Both expected developer hosts fail DNS resolution: `developer.firstwestcu.ca` (NXDOMAIN) and `developer.trucooperativebank.ca` (NXDOMAIN).
- No `.well-known` documents (security.txt, openid-configuration, oauth-authorization-server, api-catalog, ai-plugin.json) are served on any host — all probes return 404.
- Not a documented FDX participant; no published 1033-style / Consumer-Driven Banking data-access endpoint.
- Third-party access to member account data today is **aggregator-based** (screen-scraping via providers such as Plaid and Flinks), the voluntary and fragmented Canadian norm while the federal Consumer-Driven Banking framework (Budget 2024 / Fall Economic Statement 2024, overseen by the FCAC) remains legislated but not yet operational.
